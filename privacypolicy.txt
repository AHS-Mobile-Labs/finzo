Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 2, 2026

Introduction

Finzo ("the App") is an offline personal finance manager developed by AHS Mobile Labs. This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles your information.

By using Finzo, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

Information We Collect

Finzo is designed with a privacy-first approach.

The App does not collect, transmit, sell, rent, or share your personal information.

All financial data entered into Finzo, including but not limited to:

Transactions
Accounts
Budgets
Loans
Credit cards
Investments
Notes
Financial reports

is stored locally on your device.

Data Storage

Finzo stores data locally using a SQLite database.

Your financial information remains on your device and is not transmitted to AHS Mobile Labs or any third party.

You are solely responsible for managing, backing up, exporting, or deleting your data.

Internet Access

Finzo's core functionality does not require an internet connection.

The App does not upload your financial information to any cloud service operated by AHS Mobile Labs.

Analytics and Tracking

Finzo does not use:

Analytics services
User tracking
Advertising identifiers
Behavioral profiling
Data brokers

No usage statistics or financial information are collected by AHS Mobile Labs through the App.

Third-Party Services

Finzo may provide optional links to external websites such as:

GitHub
YouTube
Instagram
X (Twitter)
Email applications

When you access third-party services, their respective privacy policies and terms apply. AHS Mobile Labs is not responsible for the privacy practices of third-party websites or services.

Permissions

Finzo may request limited device permissions when required for specific features, such as:

File access for importing or exporting finance books
Storage access for backups and database management

These permissions are used solely to provide requested functionality and are not used to collect personal information.

Data Security

Your data is stored locally on your device. While Finzo takes reasonable measures to maintain data integrity, no method of electronic storage is completely secure.

Users are encouraged to regularly back up their financial data.

Children's Privacy

Finzo is not directed toward children under the age of 13.

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Open Source

Finzo is an open-source application. The source code is publicly available and can be reviewed by anyone.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

AHS Mobile Labs may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.

Updated versions will be published with a revised "Last Updated" date.
